Sobre Victoria Crimi

Hey!

I am Victoria and I am a native teacher of Spanish.

I have been teaching Spanish to young students and adults for over 9 years.

I always take into account the goal of the student with the language.

If you want to learn the basic grammar and to be able to have an informal conversation, I will plan the lessons according to that.

I usually use videos, songs and short stories to make the lesson more dynamic.

I am sure I can help you to communicate better in Spanish.

  • Ubicación: Valencia, España
  • Modalidad: Online
